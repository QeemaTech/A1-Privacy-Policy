# A1-Privacy-Policy

Last updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tamara platform, including the Tamara rider app (com.qeema.tamara.user) and the Tamara Driver partner app (com.qeema.tamara.driver). At Tamara, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.

1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how Tamara collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile applications and related services. By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information collected for all users
- Account information (name, phone number, email address)
- Location data (current location, trip routes, saved addresses)
- Payment and transaction information (wallet balance, trip payments)
- Profile information (avatar, preferences, ratings, reviews)
- Trip history and in-app communications
- Device and usage data (push notification tokens, app activity)
- Customer support interactions

2.2 Additional information — Tamara rider app
- Payment card details and billing address (when paying by card)
- Photos you upload (e.g. moments linked to places)
- Favorite places and discovery activity
- Background location on Android (when enabled, to improve ride services)

2.3 Additional information — Tamara Driver app
- Identity and driving license documents
- Vehicle type, plate number, and car photos
- Driver earnings and wallet transactions
- Online/offline availability status
- Real-time location while providing transportation services

3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:
- Provide and maintain ride-hailing, discovery, and driver partner services
- Process ride requests, payments, wallet top-ups, and driver earnings
- Match riders with drivers and enable live trip tracking
- Verify driver identity and vehicle information
- Improve and personalize your experience
- Send important updates, offers, and push notifications
- Ensure safety and security of our platform
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:
- Between riders and drivers as needed to complete trips (e.g. name, phone, pickup location, vehicle details)
- With service providers who assist us (maps, payments, cloud hosting, analytics, push messaging)
- When required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety
- With your explicit consent

5. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption of sensitive data, secure payment processing, access controls, and authentication. We never sell your personal information to third parties. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; we strive to protect your data using commercially acceptable means.

6. Your Rights
Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:
- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (including via in-app account deletion)
- Object to or restrict processing of your data
- Withdraw your consent at any time
- Data portability

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
We use cookies and similar technologies to track activity on our apps and store certain information. You can manage cookie preferences through your device or browser settings where applicable.

8. Children's Privacy
Our services are not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy and updating the "Last updated" date.
